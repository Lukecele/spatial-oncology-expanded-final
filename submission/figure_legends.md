# Manuscript Figure Legends

## Figure 1. Reaction-diffusion sensitivity scenarios

The figure shows the normalized free-drug concentration profile for the stated
lambda scenarios. The shaded interval is a conditional sub-threshold region
under the baseline assumptions; it is not a measured sanctuary, a residual-cell
map, or a clinical outcome. The 48-hour clearance curve is a sensitivity
scenario whose threshold crossing lies beyond the assumed viability boundary.

## Figure 2. ADAURA contextual dynamics

The figure reproduces source-reported ADAURA recurrence timing and ctDNA lead
time as contextual information. The displayed decay parameter is model-selected
to reproduce the reported lead time and is not a directly measured patient-level
half-life. No treatment effect is estimated from this figure.

## Supplementary Figure 1. Paired Xenium treatment-state metadata

Each line connects one of five patients between treatment-naive (TN) and
progressive disease (PD) sections. Panels show tumour alveolar score,
basal/squamous score, neuroendocrine score, and PD-like tumour fraction. Values
are metadata-derived exploratory measures; lines are paired by patient and do
not represent independent cell-level replicates.

## Supplementary Figure 2. snRNA sample-level state metadata

Boxplots show sample-level tumour-nuclei fraction and mean detected features for
TN, minimal residual disease (MRD), and PD samples. Boxes summarize samples, not
individual nuclei. The detected-feature panel is a quality/composition measure
and is not interpreted as a resistance endpoint.

## Planned supplementary analysis: spatial replication context

The replication workflow compares detection fractions for candidate features
across GSE301973 and six GSE189487 spatial samples. This figure is not included
in the current submission render. If generated in a later revision, features
should be classified as broadly detectable, sparse/platform-sensitive, or not
replicated; the analysis would remain descriptive and would not establish a
clinical biomarker.

## Exploratory score figure set

The following figures are generated by
`scripts/plot_exploratory_score_application.py` and support the exploratory
public-cohort manuscript.

### Figure 3. Melanoma exploratory score across GSE99898 states

`figures/exploratory_scores/gse99898_exploratory_score.png`

Distribution of the frozen melanoma MAPK-bypass/stress-escape score across PRE,
EDT, and PROG samples. Points represent samples and the zero line is the fixed
exploratory threshold. This is descriptive and does not establish progression
prediction.

### Figure 4. Patient-matched PRE-to-PROG score changes in GSE99898

`figures/exploratory_scores/gse99898_paired_deltas.png`

Patient-level score changes between PRE and PROG samples. The deltas use
within-dataset gene-wise standardization and should not be interpreted as a
treatment effect.

### Figure 5. Melanoma exploratory score across GSE196434 states

`figures/exploratory_scores/gse196434_exploratory_score.png`

Distribution of the frozen melanoma score across PRE and POST samples. The
state labels and zero threshold are retained as descriptive references.

### Figure 6. Pancreatic exploratory score across PRINCE arms

`figures/exploratory_scores/prince_exploratory_score.png`

Distribution of the frozen pancreatic stromal/immune-exclusion score by PRINCE
treatment arm. Two samples with unknown arm are retained in the report but are
excluded from arm-specific interpretation. This is not a causal comparison.
